# Candidate manuscript insertions: three-scene image C deletion

**AUTHOR_REVIEW / DO_NOT_SUBMIT. Separate evidence addendum; not applied to the submitted manuscript.**

These paragraphs may supplement the existing individual-component results and limitations after author review. They have not been inserted into or compiled with the submitted manuscript. The [full author-review response](E:/OneDrive/文档/GuardFed/docs/server_deployment_20260923/revision_20260923/rebuttal_integrated_C20_20261009/rebuttal_integrated_20261009.md) and all its original comments remain unchanged.

**Individual C-deletion results.** We compare 30 minus_C checkpoints with 30 paired historical Full checkpoints in exactly three IID CelebA scenes: Benign, F Flip and FedSA, ten matched seeds per scene. Deleting C removes its score contribution while retaining geometric hard filtering; it does not remove prediction calibration. Each model's raw, native and shared-calibration metrics use its same round-70 checkpoint. For IID FedSA, ten-seed paired ACC/AEOD/ASPD differences (minus_C−Full) are +0.279 ± 0.498 / -0.00024 ± 0.00472 / +0.00099 ± 0.01311 in native/shared and +0.235 ± 0.535 / -0.00132 ± 0.01034 / +0.00142 ± 0.01245 in raw, with ACC differences in percentage points and all entries mean ± sample SD (ddof=1). Deletion therefore raises native/shared ACC and ASPD while slightly lowering AEOD in this panel. Its native/shared AEOD and ASPD directions reverse in the fixed nine-/six-seed panels; raw nine-seed deletion improves all three means, whereas the raw six-seed panel raises ACC and both gaps. The [accepted three-scene table](E:/OneDrive/文档/GuardFed/docs/server_deployment_20260923/training_20260923/celeba_mechanism_v1/three_view_C_three_scenes_20261009/snapshot/TABLES.md) retains every Full/C mean, paired difference and the fixed 10/9/6-seed panels, including unfavorable outcomes.

**Trade-offs and limits.** The earlier F Flip counterexample remains: ten-seed native/shared paired differences are +0.518 ± 0.814 / +0.00139 ± 0.01487 / +0.01098 ± 0.02176, so deletion improves accuracy while increasing both disparity means. Its native/shared AEOD difference is +0.00025 ± 0.01530 for nine seeds and -0.00291 ± 0.01773 for six seeds. The Benign and earlier tabular/U negative results remain unchanged. Native/shared metrics and group counts coincide for all 60 C30 records, so the views are not independent replications. Full replay uses two CPU and 28 GPU records, while all 30 minus_C replays use CPU; both training cohorts use PyTorch 2.11.0+cu128. The U100 and nine-method historical cu130/driver disclosures remain in force. AEOD is the absolute TPR gap, not full equalized odds.

The ten-seed panel includes selection seed 91001; the nine-seed panel excludes it and the six-seed panel retains 91005–91010 identically for both procedures. Prior validation and official-test exposure remain. These 19,867-image validation results are descriptive evidence of scene-, prediction-rule- and subset-dependent trade-offs, not an untouched test, component-necessity finding or causal explanation. Six accepted IID S-DFA C records remain separately preserved and excluded from the means. The remaining seven C scenes and six other image-control variants are incomplete; the accepted U100 evidence is unchanged. The native/shared primary endpoint, final evaluation and manuscript integration remain author decisions. No additional training, model inference, threshold fitting or statistical selection was performed for these paragraphs.
