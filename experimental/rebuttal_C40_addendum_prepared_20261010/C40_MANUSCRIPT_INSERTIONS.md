**Individual C deletion.** The [accepted four-scene table](E:/OneDrive/文档/GuardFed/docs/server_deployment_20260923/training_20260923/celeba_mechanism_v1/three_view_C_four_scenes_20261010/snapshot/TABLES.md) pairs 40 minus_C checkpoints with 40 historical Full checkpoints in IID Benign, F Flip, FedSA and S-DFA, giving 80 unique records. Deleting C removes its score contribution while retaining geometric hard filtering; prediction calibration remains enabled. All three metrics and raw/native/shared-calibration views use each model's same round-70 checkpoint. For IID S-DFA, the ten-seed paired ACC/AEOD/ASPD differences (minus_C−Full) are -0.077 ± 0.715 / +0.00050 ± 0.00760 / +0.00022 ± 0.01411 in native/shared and -0.035 ± 0.715 / +0.00200 ± 0.00936 / +0.00144 ± 0.01139 in raw. Entries are means ± sample SD (ddof=1); ACC is percent and ΔACC is in percentage points (pp), while AEOD/ASPD are on [0,1]. AEOD is the absolute TPR gap, not full equalized odds. Deletion lowers ACC and raises both gaps in every ten-/nine-seed S-DFA view. Its six-seed native/shared ACC difference becomes +0.022 ± 0.770 while both gaps remain positive; raw still has lower ACC and larger gaps. The fixed 10/9/6 panels retain this mean-direction reversal and every unfavorable outcome.

**Trade-offs and limits.** F Flip's ten-seed native/shared differences remain +0.518 ± 0.814 / +0.00139 ± 0.01487 / +0.01098 ± 0.02176, so deletion improves accuracy while worsening both gaps; its AEOD direction reverses between the nine- and six-seed panels. The Benign trade-off, FedSA native/shared subset reversals and FedSA raw-nine-seed all-three-means counterexample remain unchanged. Native/shared metrics and group counts coincide for all 80 records, so the views are not independent replications.

The ten-seed panel includes selection seed 91001; the nine-seed panel excludes it, and the six-seed panel retains 91005–91010, identically for Full and minus_C. Prior validation and official-test exposure remain disclosed. These results use 19,867 validation images, not an untouched test or confirmation set; the final test under a frozen final protocol has not been run. The native/shared primary endpoint and final evaluation remain pending author decisions.

Full replay uses three CPU and 37 GPU records; all 40 minus_C replays use CPU. Both training cohorts contain 40 checkpoints from PyTorch 2.11.0+cu128. Historical U100 and nine-method cu130/CUDA/driver disclosures remain in force; historical/current driver equality was not established. Mixed replay devices and selection history limit attribution.

Only four IID C scenes are complete; six other C scenes and six other image-control variants remain incomplete. U is the sole complete image control in this snapshot, and its U100 evidence remains unchanged. These descriptive validation results do not establish C necessity, causality, significance or universal benefit. The native/shared endpoint, final evaluation and manuscript integration remain author decisions. No training, inference, threshold fitting or statistical selection was performed for these paragraphs.
