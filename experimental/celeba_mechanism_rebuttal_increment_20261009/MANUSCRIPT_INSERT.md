## English insertion

**Image mechanism evidence at the intermediate checkpoint.** We pair 60 CelebA models trained without the utility-score contribution U with 60 historical Full terminal models in six complete scenes: the five IID scenarios and non-IID Benign. Every scene has ten matched seeds, and all three metrics and prediction views for a model use the same round-70 checkpoint. In the ten-seed native panel, removing U lowers mean ACC by 0.309–1.384 percentage points, but lowers ASPD in all six scenes; AEOD rises in five scenes and falls for IID Sp-DFA. The raw outputs instead have lower AEOD after deletion in five scenes. Full therefore retains an accuracy advantage in this panel, without a uniform advantage on both disparities. The [per-scene means, sample SDs and paired differences](E:/OneDrive/文档/GuardFed/tmp/celeba_mechanism_rebuttal_increment_20261009/evidence_excerpt.md) make these trade-offs explicit. Native and shared-calibration saved metrics and confusion counts coincide for all 120 displayed models, so this subset supplies no independent calibration-gain evidence. It also cannot establish that the other components are necessary or identify candidate compensation, redundancy or root-estimation error as the cause. The nine-seed panel excluding recipe-selection seed 91001 and the six-seed panel retaining 91005–91010 apply the same rules to both procedures; they are sensitivity descriptions of previously exposed validation data, not untouched confirmation sets. The stated direction counts concern the ten-seed panel only.

**Comparison boundary.** AEOD measures the absolute TPR gap, rather than full equalized odds. Native retains each procedure's original clean-root calibration; raw and shared calibration are parallel views of the same checkpoints, with root-only threshold fitting for shared calibration. The reported Full replays mix five CPU and 55 GPU models; the 60 minus_U replays use CPU. Full training includes 59 cu128 records and one cu130 record (non-IID Benign, seed 91001), while minus_U uses the current cu128/driver 595 environment; historical and current drivers were not held equal. These runtime/device differences limit causal attribution, and this valid-only intermediate snapshot is not a uniform-device final fairness comparison or a completed all-component image ablation. The native/shared main endpoint remains pending. The complete component cohort, other scene coverage and final evaluation require separate acceptance. Previously reported COMPAS counterexamples remain part of the mechanism analysis.
