Eleven methods; fixed shared seeds 91001-91010; five scenarios per distribution. Mean ± sample SD (ddof=1). ACC is percent; AEOD/ASPD are gaps in [0,1]. AEOD is the absolute TPR gap. GuardFed-AD2+ is placed last in both tables.

**Best** and <u>second-best</u> eligible displayed means are marked within each scenario and metric; displayed ties share a rank. ACC: higher is better; AEOD/ASPD: lower is better. † retains an AEOD/ASPD value whose corresponding full-precision mean ACC is <=80%; only mean ACC >80% qualifies for fairness ranking. ACC ranking is unrestricted. All ten seeds, means and SDs are retained. This display convention was author-approved on 2026-10-12 after viewing the results; it is not preregistered or a significance test.

Native validation predictions retain each method's postprocessing and documented adaptations. Training/replay runtimes are historically mixed; this is not a uniformly-CUDA or final-test table. Seed 91001 participated in validation selection, and historical validation/test metadata were exposed. Eleven methods do not complete the 17-method target. Full provenance and the fixed 9/6-seed sensitivity/aggregate views remain in ALL_PANELS_ARCHIVE.md and the unchanged scientific JSON files.
