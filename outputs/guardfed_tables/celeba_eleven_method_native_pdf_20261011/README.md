Updated at the user's request on 2026-10-12: **best displayed mean is bold**, and the second-best distinct displayed mean is underlined. Rank separately within each distribution, seed panel, scenario and metric. Higher ACC and lower AEOD/ASPD are better. Equal displayed means share a rank; SD does not determine rank. All methods use the same rule. These are descriptive marks, not significance tests.

The author then approved the CelebA utility condition: **AEOD/ASPD participate in best/second-best highlighting only if the corresponding full-precision mean ACC is strictly greater than 80%**. At ACC <=80%, retain the original fairness values and append †, with no bold or underline. ACC ranking remains unrestricted. Apply this rule separately within each of the original 10/9/6-seed panels and, for seed-first aggregates, to the corresponding aggregate mean ACC. Never filter individual seeds or recompute conditional means. This is an author-approved display convention adopted after viewing the results, not a preregistered experiment rule or a statement that the excluded disparities are mathematically undefined.
