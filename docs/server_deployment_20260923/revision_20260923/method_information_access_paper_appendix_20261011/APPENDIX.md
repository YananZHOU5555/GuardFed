**Table X. Information access and adaptation boundaries of the 14 integrated implementations.** Entries describe algorithmic inputs, not identical information budgets or completed experimental coverage. Adapted implementations are labelled explicitly; component reuse does not establish complete original-method fidelity.

The common local-Adam platform computes a clean-root reference update and evaluates uploaded models on the root even when an aggregator ignores these quantities. Training reweighting uses population-level Male×Smiling frequencies. Thus, “no root/group input” below describes the aggregation rule, not the platform. The root contains labelled training images disjoint from client samples and validation data. Here, group denotes Male, and AEOD is the absolute true-positive-rate gap, not full equalized odds.

Fed-NGA and Huber compute unweighted cross-entropy gradients at one shared model; their computation budgets and attack-injection semantics differ from local-Adam updates. FedSA/DFA attacks separately consume the declared root reference. Equal rounds therefore do not establish equal computational work.

Raw prediction uses uncalibrated outputs; native prediction preserves each method’s own rule, including LoGoFair’s fitted postprocessor. Shared calibration is a separate root-fitted group-threshold control requiring labels/groups for fitting and groups at prediction; it is not native to root-free aggregators. Validation labels support disclosed selection/reporting, not root-threshold fitting; these comparisons are not untouched-test confirmation.

FedWA, SmartFL and FedDNA remain specification gaps and receive no experimental entries here. The obtained FedWA abstract does not fix the learning specification or reward-data permissions; complete matched SmartFL and FedDNA definitions remain unavailable. Their information requirements are unknown, and unrelated methods or simplified heuristics are not substitutes.
